## Library

Load `house-kit.gltf` once with the Three.js GLTFLoader; `house-kit.bin` and the PNGs sit next to it. The default scene has one root node per piece, named exactly as the piece and its source glTF file, with the source node transform (none of the source pieces has one). Pieces are in name order.

- Materials are deduplicated by name: each kit material appears once (8 materials), and the script fails if two pieces declare different definitions under the same name.
- Images are deduplicated by URI (18 images); textures and samplers are remapped to them. Only images referenced by the subset's materials are included.
- Accessors and bufferViews of every piece are concatenated, 4-byte aligned, into one buffer. Geometry, normals, UVs and material parameters are unchanged.
- All materials are core glTF 2.0 metallic-roughness; no `extensionsUsed`/`extensionsRequired`, skins, animations, morph targets or cameras.
- Textures larger than 1024px are downscaled with sharp (lanczos3) to 1024px and re-encoded as PNG (compression 9, no palette); smaller textures would be copied byte-for-byte. No `*_Normal`/`*_Roughness` fallback to 512px was needed.
- `house-kit.gltf`: 82,834 bytes; `house-kit.bin`: 1,193,296 bytes; textures: 18,819,835 bytes.
